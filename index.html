ANVILBORN — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

ANVILBORN is a training app. This policy says what it stores, what leaves your phone, who receives it, and what you can do about it. It describes what the app actually does. When the app changes, this page changes with it.

The short version

  • You never have to give an email address. Every part of the app works without one.
  • The app makes an anonymous account for you on first launch. It has no email or name attached. It exists so your training can be backed up and so a leaderboard can have a row for you. You can delete it, and everything in it, from Settings.
  • Your training lives on your phone first. The copy on our server is a backup, never the original.
  • Photos never leave your phone, and routes only if you press Share. Progress photos and your profile and cover images stay on the device. The GPS route of a run, walk, ride or hike stays there too, unless you share that one run with your friends as a ghost, and then its first and last 300 m are cut first. In a cardio duel, the one friend you are running with sees your position live, and only until the duel ends.
  • Your friends, Shared Forges and a rival can see your name, level, title, verified activity and strongest body part. Friends you add see more, and you can turn that off. A rival you ask to be matched with sees only your name, level and days trained.
  • Nothing about you is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers. There are no ads and no advertising trackers in the app.

Who is responsible

ANVILBORN is made and run by one person, an individual developer, not a company. That developer decides what the app collects and why, and is the "controller" of your data under laws like the GDPR. The developer is named on the Google Play listing and can be reached at the address under Contact.

What is stored on your phone

  • The character you made, and its name.
  • Every session, exercise and set you log, with dates.
  • Your runs, walks, rides and hikes, with their GPS routes.
  • Your XP, level, medals, titles and training-volume figures.
  • Anything you chose to tell the app: bodyweight, height, units, date of birth, sex, equipment, places you train, training days, goal, food and protein entries, and notes.
  • Progress photos, a profile photo and a cover image, if you add them.
  • Your settings.

Only the character is needed to start, and the app asks your year of birth once to check your age; the year itself is not kept. Everything else is optional: the app plans and scores the same without it.

What leaves your phone, and why

Your training, to our database (Supabase)

A copy of your training is stored on our Supabase database, in the United States, so you can restore it or use a second phone: your character, sessions, sets, lifts you add yourself, XP, medals, plans, and the profile fields above. Each row is readable only by the account that owns it, enforced by the database itself. Photos are not part of this copy.

Your board row, to people you know or were matched with

If you are on a leaderboard, one row about you is readable by your friends, your Shared Forges and a rival: your display name, level, title, verified activity total and strongest body part. Your invite code is yours to share. Your lifts, records, bodyweight, height, birth date, notes and photos are never in it.

Checks against cheating

For the world standing, the app tells our server the type, distance, time and date of each verified activity, never its route, so impossible ones can be left out. The app can also ask Google Play whether it is a genuine copy on a genuine device, and we keep only the result. This affects only the world standing and Find a Rival.

Friend alerts

If you turn on Friend alerts, a push token for your phone is stored on our server and given to Expo and Google to deliver them. The alert itself can carry a friend’s name and what they did. Turning them off deletes the token.

What friends and rivals see

People you add with an invite code, and people in a Shared Forge with you, can see which body your character has (male or female), your training volume per body part, your days and sessions per week, the last day you trained and your current streak, your sensor-recorded distance per week and how many sessions a sensor backed. Friends can also see new lift records, any run you share as a ghost, and sessions you send them. Turn off Share with friends and this is removed from the server.

A ghost is shared one run at a time, only when you press Share. Its first and last 300 m and its time of day are removed before it leaves your phone, and Stop sharing deletes it from the server.

In a cardio duel, a race or a run together that you and a friend or your rival both accept, your phone sends that one person your distance, time, pace and position every few seconds while the duel runs. No position is sent in your first 200 m or once you are within 200 m of where you stop, so neither end of your route is shown. With the position goes the name of the area it is in, such as a neighbourhood or town, never a street: your phone looks it up with its own built-in geocoder (on Android, the Google location service) about once a minute. These live updates pass through our server without being stored. What is stored is the invite and, when you finish, your finish time, distance and whether GPS checks passed, so both of you see the same result and your race record.

If you choose Find a Rival, a stranger near your level is matched with you for one week. They see your name, level and days, sessions and sensor distance per week, and nothing else. You become friends only if both of you say so. You can block or report a rival at any time, and then they can no longer see any of this. A report records who reported whom, and three reports stop somebody being matched.

Sign-in (Supabase and Google)

If you add an email or use Google sign-in, your email address is stored with your account so you can sign in again. With Google sign-in, Google tells us your email and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password or contacts.

Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

If you buy Pro or leave a tip, Google Play takes the payment. We never see your card or bank details. RevenueCat, our purchase service, receives your anonymous account identifier and the purchase record from Google Play (what you bought, when, and whether it is active) so the app knows you have Pro on any phone.

Health Connect, only if you connect it

Health Connect is used only if you tap Connect, and only for the switches you leave on. Each one does one thing:

  • Weight, height: shown to you, and copied into your profile only when you tap Fill my profile.
  • Body fat: shown on Status. Not stored.
  • Protein (nutrition): added to your Fuel log, one total per day. Backed up like the rest of your log.
  • Steps: shown as this week’s total on Integrations. Not stored.
  • Distance and exercise sessions: turned into verified activity XP, which is backed up and is what a leaderboard ranks on. The readings themselves are not uploaded.
  • Writing: your finished sessions, an estimate of the calories they used, and weight and height when you change them in the app.

You can withdraw any of it at any time in Health Connect. Health Connect data is used only to run these features, never for advertising, never to make decisions about you, and never sold.

Location, only while you record cardio

When you start a run, walk, ride or hike, the app asks for your location and uses GPS until you finish, with a notification showing the whole time. It never tracks you in the background otherwise. Your route stays on this phone: it is not uploaded, not in backups, and deleted with your data. Only the distance and the XP it earned are backed up. The one exception is a cardio duel, below, where the friend you are running with sees where you are while it runs.

Route maps, only if you allow it. While you record, and in the route flyover, the app can show your route over a map (in 3D in the flyover). The first time, the app asks; the one answer covers both. If you allow it, map tiles are downloaded from OpenFreeMap, and terrain from Amazon Web Services' public terrain data, for the area around your route, so those services see your IP address and roughly where the route was. Map areas you have viewed, and the areas around your home forge and saved routes, are kept on the phone so the map also works without a connection. Your route itself is never sent: it is drawn on the phone. If you decline, the app draws the route itself and no map service is contacted. You can change this in the flyover at any time. Typed-in indoor sessions have no route and use no map.

The Wear OS watch app, only if you install it

If you install the watch app, your current lift, set, weight and rest time pass from your phone to the watch, and the sets you log on the watch pass back to the phone. They travel through Google's Wear OS Data Layer, which can carry them through Google's servers when the watch and phone are apart. Nothing else uses them.

Analytics and crash reports (PostHog and Sentry)

  • PostHog receives events about how the app is used, such as that a set was logged or a level was reached, with your anonymous account identifier. It never receives your name, email, a photo or a location: the app's event schema forbids those fields, and a test enforces it.
  • Sentry receives crash reports so bugs can be fixed. It is set up not to attach personal information.
  • Something wrong? in Settings sends what you type there to Sentry, with a few app details such as the version and whether your backup is up to date. Only send what you are happy for us to read.

App updates and downloads

The app checks Expo’s update service for new versions, and downloads the offline coach’s model from Hugging Face if you choose to. Like any download, these services see your IP address and device type. Nothing about your training is sent.

What stays on the phone

  • The coach runs entirely on your phone. Your questions never leave it.
  • Spoken countdowns use your phone’s own text-to-speech.
  • Rest-timer and reminder notifications are scheduled on the phone.
  • Home screen widgets, the session on the lock screen and the daily session card are drawn on the phone from what the app saves there. Anyone holding your phone can read its lock screen, so Workout settings can hide weights there, and both can be turned off.
  • The app sets no cookies and contains no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the app you asked for (contract): storing and backing up your training, sign-in, Pro, leaderboards.
  • With your consent: Health Connect, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: crash reports and anonymous usage events, to keep the app working and improve it. You can object by emailing us.
  • Legal obligations: keeping purchase records where tax law requires.

Health information

Training logs, bodyweight and activity can count as health-related data in some countries. We use them only to run the app for you, never for advertising, and never sell them.

Nothing in this app is medical advice or a health assessment. The body-part figures are an index of how much training each part has had. They are not a statement about your body, your fitness or your health. Talk to a qualified professional about your health.

How long data is kept

  • Your backup and account: until you delete them. Deleting your account removes them at once.
  • Analytics events and crash reports: only as long as needed to fix and improve the app, under those services’ retention settings.
  • Purchase records: as long as Google Play, RevenueCat and tax law require.
  • On your phone: until you delete the data, the account, or the app.

Where data is processed

Our services are mainly in the United States. If you live elsewhere, your data is transferred there. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on the providers’ standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

Children

ANVILBORN is for people aged 16 and over. The app asks your year of birth before setting anything up and does not let anyone younger continue. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, email us and we will delete their data.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can do all of this:

  • Get a copy of your data. Settings exports your whole history as a dated file through your phone's share sheet.
  • Delete your account and everything in it, from Settings → Your data. It removes the server copy, the account and this phone’s database straight away. Without the app, follow cma-uplink.github.io/anvilborn-legal/delete.html.
  • Correct your data by editing it in the app.
  • Withdraw consent for Health Connect in Health Connect.
  • Leave a leaderboard by removing the friendship, or delete the account to leave every board. Stop sharing with friends in Friends.
  • Ask us anything, or object to analytics, by email.

If you are in the EU, UK, or a country with similar law (such as Switzerland, Brazil, Canada, South Africa, Australia or Japan), you also have the rights your law gives: access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your data protection authority. If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law, you can know, delete and correct your data. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising, and we do not use it to profile you for decisions with legal effects. We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights. We answer requests within 30 days.

Security

Everything sent to our services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each account can read only its own rows on the server. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Who processes what

  • Supabase: backup, accounts and leaderboard rows. United States.
  • RevenueCat: purchase records.
  • Google (Google Play, Google sign-in, Health Connect, Wear OS): payments, sign-in, the activity data you choose to share, and what passes between your phone and watch.
  • PostHog: anonymous product analytics.
  • Sentry: crash reports.
  • Expo: app updates.
  • OpenFreeMap (map tiles, from OpenStreetMap data) and Amazon Web Services (public terrain data): map and terrain downloads for the cardio maps and the 3D flyover, only if you allow it.

They act for us, on our instructions. None is given your data for its own advertising, and none is an advertising network.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. If a change ever means something new leaves your phone, the app will ask you before it does.

Contact

Questions, requests about your data, or complaints: cma.apps.official@gmail.com. Settings can export or delete your data without waiting on a reply.
