ANVILBORN — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

ANVILBORN is a training app. This policy says what it stores, what leaves your phone, who receives it, and what you can do about it. It describes what the app actually does. When the app changes, this page changes with it.

The short version

  • You never have to give an email address. Every part of the app works without one.
  • The app makes an anonymous account for you on first launch. It has no email or name attached. It exists so your training can be backed up and so a leaderboard can have a row for you. You can delete it, and everything in it, from Settings.
  • Your training lives on your phone first. The copy on our server is a backup, never the original.
  • Photos never leave your phone. Progress photos, your profile photo and your cover image stay on the device. That is enforced in the code and by a test.
  • Other people can see six things about you, and only if you appear on a leaderboard: your name, your level, your title, your verified activity, your strongest body part, and your invite code.
  • Nothing about you is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers. There are no ads and no advertising trackers in the app.

Who is responsible

ANVILBORN is made and run by one person, an individual developer, not a company. That developer decides what the app collects and why, and is the "controller" of your data under laws like the GDPR. The developer is named on the Google Play listing and can be reached at the address under Contact.

What is stored on your phone

  • The character you made, and its name.
  • Every session, exercise and set you log, with dates.
  • Your XP, level, medals, titles and training-volume figures.
  • Anything you chose to tell the app: bodyweight, height, units, date of birth, sex, equipment, places you train, training days, goal, food and protein entries, and notes.
  • Progress photos, a profile photo and a cover image, if you add them.
  • Your settings.

Only the character is needed to start, and the app asks your year of birth once to check your age; the year itself is not kept. Everything else is optional: the app plans and scores the same without it.

What leaves your phone, and why

Your training, to our database (Supabase)

A copy of your training is stored on our Supabase database, in the United States, so you can restore it or use a second phone: your character, sessions, sets, XP, medals, plans, and the profile fields above. Each row is readable only by the account that owns it, enforced by the database itself. Photos are not part of this copy.

Six facts, to anyone on a leaderboard with you

If you are on a leaderboard, one row about you is readable by other users: your display name, level, title, verified activity total, strongest body part and invite code. Your lifts, records, bodyweight, height, birth date, notes and photos are never in it.

Sign-in (Supabase and Google)

If you add an email or use Google sign-in, your email address is stored with your account so you can sign in again. With Google sign-in, Google tells us your email and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password or contacts.

Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

If you buy Pro or leave a tip, Google Play takes the payment. We never see your card or bank details. RevenueCat, our purchase service, receives your anonymous account identifier and the purchase record from Google Play (what you bought, when, and whether it is active) so the app knows you have Pro on any phone.

Health Connect, only if you connect it

If you tap Connect, the app can read steps, distance and exercise sessions from Health Connect, and nothing else. It asks only when you tap Connect. The readings are not uploaded. The XP they earn is stored as verified activity and backed up like your other training, and that total is what a leaderboard ranks on. You can withdraw access any time in Health Connect. Health Connect data is used only to run these features, never for advertising, and never sold.

Analytics and crash reports (PostHog and Sentry)

  • PostHog receives events about how the app is used, such as that a set was logged or a level was reached, with your anonymous account identifier. It never receives your name, email, a photo or a location: the app's event schema forbids those fields, and a test enforces it.
  • Sentry receives crash reports so bugs can be fixed. It is set up not to attach personal information.

App updates and downloads

The app checks Expo’s update service for new versions, and downloads the offline coach’s model from Hugging Face if you choose to. Like any download, these services see your IP address and device type. Nothing about your training is sent.

What stays on the phone

  • The coach runs entirely on your phone. Your questions never leave it.
  • Spoken countdowns use your phone’s own text-to-speech.
  • Rest-timer and reminder notifications are scheduled on the phone.
  • The app sets no cookies and contains no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the app you asked for (contract): storing and backing up your training, sign-in, Pro, leaderboards.
  • With your consent: Health Connect, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: crash reports and anonymous usage events, to keep the app working and improve it. You can object by emailing us.
  • Legal obligations: keeping purchase records where tax law requires.

Health information

Training logs, bodyweight and activity can count as health-related data in some countries. We use them only to run the app for you, never for advertising, and never sell them.

Nothing in this app is medical advice or a health assessment. The body-part figures are an index of how much training each part has had. They are not a statement about your body, your fitness or your health. Talk to a qualified professional about your health.

How long data is kept

  • Your backup and account: until you delete them. Deleting your account removes them at once.
  • Analytics events and crash reports: only as long as needed to fix and improve the app, under those services’ retention settings.
  • Purchase records: as long as Google Play, RevenueCat and tax law require.
  • On your phone: until you delete the data, the account, or the app.

Where data is processed

Our services are mainly in the United States. If you live elsewhere, your data is transferred there. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on the providers’ standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

Children

ANVILBORN is for people aged 16 and over. The app asks your year of birth before setting anything up and does not let anyone younger continue. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, email us and we will delete their data.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can do all of this:

  • Get a copy of your data. Settings exports your whole history as a dated file through your phone's share sheet.
  • Delete your account and everything in it, from Settings → Your data. It removes the server copy, the account and this phone’s database straight away. Without the app, follow cma-uplink.github.io/anvilborn-legal/delete.html.
  • Correct your data by editing it in the app.
  • Withdraw consent for Health Connect in Health Connect.
  • Leave a leaderboard by removing the friendship, or delete the account to leave every board.
  • Ask us anything, or object to analytics, by email.

If you are in the EU, UK, or a country with similar law (such as Switzerland, Brazil, Canada, South Africa, Australia or Japan), you also have the rights your law gives: access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your data protection authority. If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law, you can know, delete and correct your data. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising, and we do not use it to profile you for decisions with legal effects. We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights. We answer requests within 30 days.

Security

Everything sent to our services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each account can read only its own rows on the server. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Who processes what

  • Supabase: backup, accounts and leaderboard rows. United States.
  • RevenueCat: purchase records.
  • Google (Google Play, Google sign-in, Health Connect): payments, sign-in, and the activity data you choose to share.
  • PostHog: anonymous product analytics.
  • Sentry: crash reports.
  • Expo: app updates.

They act for us, on our instructions. None is given your data for its own advertising, and none is an advertising network.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. If a change ever means something new leaves your phone, the app will ask you before it does.

Contact

Questions, requests about your data, or complaints: cma.apps.official@gmail.com. Settings can export or delete your data without waiting on a reply.
