ANVILBORN — Delete your account and data

ANVILBORN is a training app made by an individual developer and published on Google Play. This page explains how to delete your ANVILBORN account and the data that goes with it.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ANVILBORN.
  2. Go to Settings → Your data.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm twice.

Deletion happens straight away. There is no waiting period and no undo.

Without the app

Email cma.apps.official@gmail.com with the subject Delete my ANVILBORN account.

So we delete the right account, send it from the email address you signed in with, or include your character name and your invite code (shown on the leaderboard screen). We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in (including the email address, if you added one).
  • Everything backed up on our server: your character, profile, sessions, sets, XP, medals, plans, challenges and notes.
  • Your leaderboard entry and friend links.
  • When you delete in the app, the app's data on that phone too. Progress photos never leave the phone; uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records (what was bought and when) are kept by Google Play and our purchase service, RevenueCat, for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions.
  • Crash reports and anonymous usage events are not linked to your name or email, and expire under those services' retention settings.

Deleting only some data

You can delete individual sessions, photos and notes in the app, leave a leaderboard by removing a friendship, or disconnect Health Connect in Health Connect itself. To ask for anything else to be deleted, email the address above.
